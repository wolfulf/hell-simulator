# Hell Simulator

A 3D browser game that simulates the hell from Steven L. Peck's novella *A Short Stay in Hell*: two facing walls of bookshelves across a bottomless shaft, holding every book that can be written on 410 pages of 40 lines with 80 characters each (95 possible characters).

- The library is simulated at its full size. Floor and section numbers have about 1.3 million digits.
- Every book can be pulled and read. Its content is computed from its address, so the same place always holds the same book.
- Real words in the gibberish are highlighted. Your longest find is your record.
- Lights go out at 10 p.m. and come on at 6 a.m. If you die, you wake up the next morning.

Controls: WASD to walk, Shift to run, mouse to look, E to interact, Space to jump, Tab for finds, H for the rules, M to mute. English by default, German available on the start sign.

This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with the author or publisher of the novella.
